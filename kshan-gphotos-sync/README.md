# GP Sync (Umbrel app)

This folder is what Umbrel installs: `docker-compose.yml`, `umbrel-app.yml`, the icon and hooks.
The app itself (code, tests, Docker image and releases) lives in its own repo,
[kshanxs/gp-sync](https://github.com/kshanxs/gp-sync).

GP Sync copies every photo and video in your Umbrel **Files → Photos** and **Files → Videos**
folders to Google Photos in original quality. Nothing on your Umbrel is changed or deleted.
